ajaxed-forms — local demo

⏱️ The script attaches handlers 2 seconds after load. Wait a beat before submitting, or the form will post normally.

1. Custom action → success

Posts to postman-echo.com (returns 200). Expect a loading overlay, then a green message in the overlay with a Close button. Closing it reveals the reset form, ready to resubmit.

2. No action URL → error message

data-sleekcms="invalid" doesn't match the id format and there's no action. Expect a red error.

3. SleekCMS form id → constructed URL

data-sleekcms="contact-abc123" posts to https://form.sleekcms.com/contact-abc123. With a fake id it'll show an error — swap in your real id.

4. Dynamically added form (MutationObserver)

Click to inject a new form. The observer should attach the AJAX handler automatically.

5. Tall form → viewport-anchored panel

This form is intentionally taller than the screen. Scroll down, submit, and the loading/result panel stays centered in the viewport (a floating card) instead of disappearing into the middle of the form.
